# Free Editorial Checklist

## Mechanics
- typo
- punctuation
- grammar
- naming
- spacing
- number/unit formatting

## Logic
- contradiction
- unsupported claim
- vague comparison
- chronology

## Readability
- redundancy
- long sentence
- filler
- awkward phrasing
- weak transition

## Tone
- promotional wording
- repetitive scaffolding
- empty summary phrasing
